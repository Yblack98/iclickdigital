About iClick Digital Ventures

An indigenous technology holding enterprise headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria[cite: 1, 2].

iClick Digital Ventures was founded to solve deep administrative friction and human capital gaps across Nigeria and Africa[cite: 1, 2]. We operate four focused subsidiaries across public sector digitization, AI and business automation, technical skills training, and enterprise hardware supply[cite: 1, 2].

We believe that technology should be intuitive, accessible, and grounded in everyday realities[cite: 1, 2]. Whether we are helping a court registry track physical memos or training a fresh graduate in practical No-Code tools, our goal is permanent operational improvement[cite: 1, 2, 5].
