Corporate Profile & Vision

Building the Digital Backbone for African Enterprise & Governance

Headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria, iClick Digital Ventures is an integrated technology holding company driving seamless transformation across public institutions, commercial automation, and practical skill development.

Our Core Mission

To eradicate administrative friction in government and commercial institutions through secure digital registries, low-code automation, and robust hardware infrastructure while empowering the next generation of African tech professionals.

Our Long-Term Vision

To stand as Africa’s most trusted holding enterprise for public sector digitization, operational AI automation, and high-impact digital academies.
