Corporate Overview & Governance

Building Africa’s Integrated Digital Ecosystem

iClick Digital Ventures is an indigenous technology holding enterprise headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria, dedicated to institutional digitization, enterprise automation, practical tech talent development, and IoT infrastructure.

Our Vision

To be Africa’s premier catalyst for institutional efficiency, digital enablement, and technological empowerment across governance, commercial enterprise, and education.

Our Mission

To design, deploy, and scale intuitive digital systems that eliminate administrative bottlenecks, equip youth and executives with future-proof tech skills, and provision resilient physical technology infrastructure.

Our Guiding Standard

The I-C-L-I-C-K Framework

Innovation Deploying modern, low-code, and AI-driven frameworks that solve immediate challenges.
Competence Executing deployments with strict technical precision and zero disruption to statutory rules.
Leadership Setting industry benchmarks for transparent, secure public and private sector workflows.
Integrity Operating with full regulatory adherence (CAC, SCUML / EFCC, and statutory tax standards).
Client-Centricity Tailoring solutions to organizational reality rather than enforcing rigid off-the-shelf software.
Knowledge Transfer Ensuring internal staff and students are permanently equipped through our Digital Academy.
